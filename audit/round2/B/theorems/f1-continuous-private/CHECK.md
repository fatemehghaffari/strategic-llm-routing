## 8. (f1) is used in part (A): a correction to `ADVERSARY.md` Section 19 (P3, record)

`ADVERSARY.md` Section 19 calls the attribution of (f1) to part (A) a harmless over-attribution,
because "the coupling uses the same `Pi_C` in the two executions, which is an identity on one space
and not a statement about a law". `FINAL.md` Section 3.4 disagrees, and I confirm `FINAL.md` with
an explicit instance. The identity on one space is available only because each execution's `Pi_C`
is a fresh draw with a profile-independent law; if its conditional law may depend on the past,
the two executions' priorities differ and the conclusion fails.

*Instance.* `K = 2`, constant primitives (`L = 0`, so (H2) is `n >= 4`): agent `1` strategic with
`M_1 = mu_1 = 1`; agent `2` honest with `M_2 = 1`, `mu_2 = 0`. A deterministic path that visits
every cube equally often, so (X) holds and (A4f-P) holds for the seeds and selections. Priority rule,
violating only the freshness in (f1): at the first visit to any cube met after agent `1`'s first
Stage-1 pull, `Pi_C` ranks agent `1` first if and only if that pull's reward was `0`.

*Baseline.* Agent `1` delivers `1`, so the reward is `1` and agent `2` has priority at every later
cube. Both bids are exactly `1` (Bernoulli(`1`) rewards), so every later cube is a tie and agent `2`
wins it. Agent `1`'s utility is its Stage-1 pulls, worth `1 + 1 - 1 = 1` each: `O(J'_n L_n)`.

*Deviation.* Deliver `0` at the first Stage-1 pull, then follow `S^*_1`. That one cube's bid drops
to `(L_n - 1)/L_n` and agent `1` loses it; at every later cube the bids tie at `1`, agent `1` has
priority and wins at price `tau = (1 - sigma_n)_+`, delivers `tau`, is never switched on the good
event, and earns `1 + mu_1 - tau = 1 + min{sigma_n, 1}` per post-bidding round.

*Gain.* `(1 + min{sigma_n, 1})(n - O(n/J_n) - O(J'_n L_n)) - O(J'_n L_n) = Theta(n)`, against an
error budget of `O(n^{(m+2)/(m+3)} log n)`.

So a priority whose law reacts to an agent's earlier reward breaks part (A) by a linear amount, and
(f1)'s freshness is load bearing there. What is idle is only the *uniformity* of the priority law: a
fixed non-uniform law, drawn fresh, leaves the proof unchanged. `FINAL.md` states exactly this. The
adversary's suggested removal would have been an under-attribution, the unsafe direction, which is
the direction its own Section 19 says it checked for; it checked the proof's steps and not the
coupling's construction. Parts (B) and (C) are unaffected by this instance, as `FINAL.md` claims.

## 11. For the coordinator: manuscript statements that `FINAL.md` does not cover

`sections/app_contextual_private.tex` already contains a CG-MESP section for the shared-context
model with convex costs (Proposition `prop:ctx-continuum-exact`, Theorem `thm:ctx-private-continuum`
in `sections/sec4_contextual.tex`). Most of the P3 repairs of this round are already applied there
(the (A3) quantifier over randomisations with pinned fields; `E[J'_n]` by Jensen; the `J_n <= n`
proviso `n_1(d)`; the endogenous-benchmark remark). Four things there are outside what `FINAL.md`
proves or checks, and a reader should look at them before the paper relies on them:

1. **Part (i) over `B^ctx` "under exogeneity and (A3) alone"** (Proposition (i), and the proof's
   paragraph "What is conditioned on"). `FINAL.md` proves part (A) under (X) and (A4f-P), which is
   the manuscript's (A2) in full, and covers `B^ctx` only as a subclass of `B^ctx+`. The weaker
   claim needs a coupling in which the path is a function of objects shared by the two executions
   (seeds, mechanism coins, environment randomness) but may depend on past seeds; Lemma 9's proof
   does not cover that, and no adversarial pass on file has attacked it. It may well be true; it is
   unchecked.
2. **Two remainder constants in that proof treat a random count as fixed.** "`2 n_C Pr((H^*_C)^c)`"
   and "`2n Sigma_C Pr(bad_C) <= 2n . n . (...)`" are valid conditionally on the path, and the
   proof says explicitly that it conditions on no path. Without it, the first is
   `2 E[n_C 1_{(H^*_C)^c}]`, whose sum over cubes is at most `2 n Pr(H^*^c) <= 2 J_n n^{-3}/K`, and
   the second must run over the `J_n` grid cubes, not the at most `n` visited ones. Both give
   `O(n^{-2})` once `J_n <= n`, so no rate or statement changes; it is exactly the "random index
   set / random count" shape of `EXPLORER.md` D1 and should be tidied for that reason. `FINAL.md`
   Section 9 carries the correct constants (`6 r_n`).
3. **Convex costs.** The `Lambda` factors and the cost-conversion lemma `lem:cost-convert` are not
   in the audited material and were not checked here.
4. **"The leading constant is free of `K` once `n >~ (K log K)^{d+3}`"** (`thm:ctx-private-continuum`
   (i)). Consistent with `FINAL.md`'s constants: in part (A) only `e_fb` carries `K`, and its two
   pieces fall below `n^{(m+2)/(m+3)}` when `K log K << n^{1/(m+3)}`. Not a separate claim I checked
   beyond that arithmetic.
